Our Story

Cooking the way fire intended

Ember & Olive began with a single clay oven and a stubborn belief: the best food needs very little — good ingredients, patient hands and a real flame.

Every morning our chefs meet local farmers and fishermen, then build the day's menu around what's at its peak. Nothing is rushed, nothing is hidden, and everything touches the fire.
